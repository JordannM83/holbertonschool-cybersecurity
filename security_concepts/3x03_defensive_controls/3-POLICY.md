# Acceptable Use Policy

> **Section 4.2: Prohibited Activities**
>
> Users shall not use BioHealth systems, devices, networks, accounts, or other company resources for cryptocurrency mining or any other activity that consumes company resources for personal gain. Users shall not install, download, or execute software, scripts, applications, or services without prior authorization from the appropriate BioHealth IT or security authority. These activities are strictly prohibited, including when performed outside normal business hours or on a company server that is not actively supporting production work.
>
> Violation of this section may result in immediate suspension or termination of employment or contract, removal of system access, and referral to law enforcement or other legal action where appropriate. BioHealth reserves the right to investigate suspected violations and recover costs or damages resulting from them.
