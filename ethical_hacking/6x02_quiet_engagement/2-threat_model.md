# Threat Model, Lumen Industrial Systems

## Framework Mix

This model combines PASTA business-impact framing with STRIDE decomposition and MITRE ATT&CK technique tags. PASTA keeps the prioritisation tied to Lumen’s NIS2 conformity evidence, customer renewals, and industrial-monitoring availability; STRIDE distinguishes identity, integrity, disclosure, availability, and privilege risks across the cloud and application trust boundaries; and ATT&CK tags the concrete externally exposed attack paths that reconnaissance is intended to validate. The assets named below are the Lumen-controlled asset classes surfaced in the engagement brief and approved RoE; no customer-owned asset is treated as a threat target.

## Prioritisation Note

Threats 2, 11, and 1 are the top three business risks. Cross-tenant access (2) could expose several industrial customers through one SaaS weakness and would be difficult to defend in a NIS2 conformity packet. A spoofed or tampered gateway/MQTT trust path (11) threatens the integrity of the monitoring data that customers rely on, even if the cloud perimeter remains intact. Public API compromise (1) is the most direct external route into Lumen’s platform and could combine confidentiality, integrity, and availability impact. These three findings would put the three customer renewals and the Series A condition at greatest risk; their severity must be confirmed by controlled testing and evidence, not inferred from the threat model alone.
